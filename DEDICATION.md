# Dedication

**Bob Wright** — creator of RC Crew Chief (RC3) — died in 2023.

For more than a decade he did something rare in this hobby: he published the physics.
While the rest of us traded setup sheets and folklore, Bob measured the car, wrote down the
equations, built the tool, and then taught it — a ten-part *Science of Setup* series, a
seven-part motor-dyno series, and walkthroughs for modelling a car from a set of calipers
and a flat plate. He answered questions on the RC Tech thread himself, in detail, for
hundreds of pages, and he told people plainly when his model could not do something.

He also made the app **free** in January 2023, with the words *"free but not open source."*

## What this project takes, and what it does not

| | |
|---|---|
| **Takes** | The physics (Newton, Euler and standard vehicle dynamics), his *published teaching* — cited and linked, never republished wholesale — and the workflow of measure → model → compare. |
| **Asks for** | The car/damper/motor model data, by the family's gift, if they are willing. It is the one part of RC3 that cannot be rebuilt from first principles. |
| **Does not take** | His source code, binaries, help text, iconography, or the names "RC Crew Chief" / "RC3". |

We do not decompile his binary. It would be quick, it would be legal to argue about, and it
would poison the only thing this project actually needs: his family's goodwill.

## Standing thanks

- **Bob Wright's family**, who told the RC Tech community that keeping his work alive "is
  what his father would have wanted", and who have been sent our own request in writing.
- The **RC Tech community**, who kept a dead Windows app installable for three years and
  rescued the model library from the Wayback Machine.
- The chassis modellers whose geometry we re-derive: the Awesomatix model was originally
  contributed by a community member and rewritten by Bob.

If the family ever wants this project renamed, re-badged, or handed over, we do that
without argument.
