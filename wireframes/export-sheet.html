  • One validity state per value, as the same hollow ring used in the app, plus one sentence in words at the foot.
  • Provenance travels: measured, estimated (with its stored tolerance), inherited (with the source).
  • No tolerance is invented on the way out. An estimate with no recorded tolerance prints with ~ and nothing else.
  • When bench evidence exists the same slot reads validated and names the case — the layout does not change, so old sheets stay comparable.
