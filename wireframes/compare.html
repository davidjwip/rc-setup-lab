Two setups, one column of arrows. The screen is a reading instrument: aligned digits down a column, one emphasised column, and a written sentence that says what the difference will feel like.

  • Columns are locked to 66 px so digits line up down the page. Tabular figures, right aligned, always.
  • Delta is the third column, never a colour on the value itself — the number stays readable, the arrow carries the direction.
  • Unchanged rows stay available but out of the way behind “Changed only”. Nothing is silently filtered.
  • The summary sentence is the point of the screen: three changes in, one handling consequence out.
  • Units live with the property label; there is no room for a unit in every cell at 390 px.
Tap the swap icon: A and B exchange and every delta flips sign, which is how a racer checks the direction of a change before applying it.
