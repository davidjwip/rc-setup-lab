Measured geometry

Model once, from real measurements. This is the slowest screen in the app, so it is the one that has to feel calm, ordered and recoverable — one corner, one hardpoint at a time.

  • A pinned front-view schematic is the map. Tapping a row lights its dot, so the field and the part are never separated.
  • Hardpoints are 3D, but a racer thinks in three plain directions: forward from the axle, out from the centreline, up from the floor. Those are the field labels, not X/Y/Z alone.
  • Measure one corner, mirror the other — a switch, not a duplication chore.
  • Progress is stated honestly (31 of 42) with a time estimate, because the user needs to know whether to start now or after the next heat.
Datum rule: X is measured from the front axle centreline of that end. If a car is measured from a different datum, every downstream number is wrong — so the datum is always named on screen.
