Inferredsteady-state reasoning from the firm rows above

More front roll-stiffness share means more front lateral load transfer, so the front axle carries proportionally more load and the car understeers more mid-corner, all else equal.

The extra 0.2° of front camber holds the front tyre flatter at that load, which pulls the other way — also mid-corner, also all else equal. Which one wins is not something this model can say.

inference · grip

With more load on the front pair at the same slip angle, the front is the axle more likely to run out of grip first, mid-corner — all else equal. The rear is asked for correspondingly less.

Not measured, not computed, no number and no badge: grip is a tyre property this build does not model. Turning this into a claim needs tyre data or a measured test — neither is in v1.

Reverses if

The front tyres are already past peak slip at this load, or the rear gains more camber than the front through the same roll angle. Both are tyre and geometry facts the app does not hold.

What it does not claim

Grip, lap time, or a number of degrees of understeer. It claims where the load went, not what the tyre did with it.

Speculativeneeds a transient model or a measurement the app does not have

Entry and exit are not modelled. Turn-in sharpness, how fast the car takes a set, and corner-exit traction all come from damper forces and the rate of load transfer — neither is in v1.

Sheet diff + interpreter

Two sheets, grouped by area, then a reading in three labelled tiers. The tier is the honesty mechanism: it says how far the physics actually reaches, per claim.

  • Firm — geometry, rates and their arithmetic. The app stands behind direction and magnitude.
  • Inferred — steady-state balance direction, and every such sentence carries all else equal on screen, because the inference depends on the tyre staying out of it.
  • Speculative — entry, exit, rates of response. Named, not answered, and never dressed as an estimate.
  • A rule with no tier does not ship, so the panel has no unlabelled sentence.
  • Conditions are optional per sheet — surface, temperature, humidity, free text for tyres and motor — and the comparability banner sits above the tiers, never below them.
Three tiers, not four: a confidence percentage would pretend the inference has a measurable width, and a fourth band would only repeat Speculative under a different name. The phase axis (entry / mid / exit) is a tag on each rule, not a tier.
