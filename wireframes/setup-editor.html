Setup editor

Four groups, one thumb, nothing else. A change should be one gesture and the consequence should be visible on the same row — never a separate screen to check whether it worked.

  • The number is the control. Drag the rail or tap the number to type; there is no duplicate stepper next to every slider.
  • Every rate row carries its own consequence underneath: wheel rate, motion ratio, share of roll stiffness.
  • Discrete values (oil, piston holes) are chips, not sliders. Continuous values are sliders. Never mix.
  • Units are always visible next to the number, at 12 px, and never inside the input.
  • The save bar states how many changes are pending; nothing is auto-saved silently over a baseline setup.
Tabs switch groups in place — no nested navigation, no page push. The four groups are the whole editor.
