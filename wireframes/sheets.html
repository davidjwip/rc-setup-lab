Setup sheets

A sheet is a whole setup at a moment: values, conditions, a note. The library is the car's memory, and the diff is what turns two memories into a reading.

  • Newest first, with the baseline pinned and visible. A sheet is never overwritten: saving creates a new one.
  • Any two sheets can be compared, not just consecutive ones — a diff spans a whole season.
  • An imported sheet is marked inherited with the name of the person or template it came from.
  • Conditions travel with the sheet, so a diff can say “different day, not comparable” before it says anything else.
The empty state is the honest one: no sheets, no numbers, and two explicit ways to start.
